## Business Context

- A hospital generates data every time a patient comes in and goes through the admission process. This data can help the hospital understand the people it is serving, the diseases they are coming in with, the severity of their conditions, the wards being used and how long patients stay.

- My understanding is that the hospital manager would want reliable information to see where the hospital is doing well and where improvements may be needed. For the data analyst to provide reliable reports, the underlying data needs to be clean and accurate.
